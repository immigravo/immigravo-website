    How to read the Visa Bulletin

    Each month the Department of State publishes a Visa Bulletin for family- and employment-based green cards that have annual limits. Find the row for your category and the column for your country of birth, then compare the date shown with your priority date.

    CategoryWho it covers
    F1Unmarried sons and daughters (21+) of U.S. citizens
    F2A / F2BSpouses and children / unmarried adult children of green card holders
    F3 / F4Married children / brothers and sisters of U.S. citizens
    EB-1 / EB-2 / EB-3Priority workers / advanced-degree professionals / skilled workers
    • “C” means current: anyone in that category can move forward.
    • A date means only applicants with a priority date before it can move forward.
    • “Final action dates” decide when a green card can be issued; “dates for filing” decide when you may submit paperwork.
