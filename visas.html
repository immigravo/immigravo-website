U.S. visa types at a glance

A plain-English overview of the most common routes. Use the switch to compare permanent and temporary options.

IR-1 / CR-1
Spouse of a U.S. citizen

For husbands and wives of U.S. citizens. An immediate-relative category, so no annual cap applies.

FamilyGreen card
IR-2 / IR-5
Children and parents of U.S. citizens

For unmarried children under 21, and for parents of U.S. citizens who are 21 or older.

FamilyGreen card
F1 – F4
Family preference

For other relatives, such as adult children and siblings of citizens, or spouses and children of green card holders. Subject to annual limits.

FamilyGreen card
EB-1
Priority workers

For people with extraordinary ability, outstanding professors and researchers, and certain multinational managers.

EmploymentGreen card
EB-2 / EB-3
Professionals and skilled workers

For advanced-degree professionals, people of exceptional ability and skilled workers, usually with an employer sponsor.

EmploymentGreen card
EB-5
Immigrant investors

For investors who put qualifying capital into a U.S. business that creates jobs for U.S. workers.

InvestmentGreen card
DV
Diversity Visa lottery

An annual lottery for people from countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States.

LotteryGreen card
K-1
Fiancé(e) of a U.S. citizen

Lets a fiancé(e) enter the U.S. to marry within 90 days and then apply for a green card.

FamilyLeads to green card
B-1 / B-2
Visitor

For short business trips, tourism, family visits or medical treatment. Some nationalities can use ESTA instead.

VisitTemporary
F-1 / M-1
Student

For full-time academic (F-1) or vocational (M-1) study at a school approved to enrol international students.

StudyTemporary
J-1
Exchange visitor

For approved exchange programmes, including researchers, interns, au pairs and visiting scholars.

ExchangeTemporary
H-1B
Specialty occupation

For roles that require at least a bachelor's degree, sponsored by a U.S. employer. Subject to an annual cap for most employers.

WorkTemporary
L-1
Intracompany transferee

For managers, executives and specialised staff moving to a U.S. office of the same company.

WorkTemporary
O-1
Extraordinary ability

For people with sustained national or international acclaim in sciences, arts, education, business or athletics.

WorkTemporary
TN
USMCA professional

For Canadian and Mexican citizens in certain listed professions working for a U.S. employer.

WorkTemporary
E-2
Treaty investor

For nationals of treaty countries who invest a substantial amount in a U.S. business they will direct.

InvestmentTemporary
